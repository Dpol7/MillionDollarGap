BUILD THE COMPLETE "LOCK YOUR BLOCK" USER EXPERIENCE

I want the process of claiming a Bitcoin block to feel simple, exciting, and permanent.

The core message is:

PICK YOUR BLOCK. LOCK IT IN.

A user should be able to explore the map freely, select different blocks, and see their estimated timing before committing.

STEP 1 — USER EXPLORES THE MAP

The user lands on the map and sees all available blocks.

Available blocks are gray.

Claimed blocks are orange.

The user's eventual block will be green.

When the user hovers over a block:

The dot becomes slightly larger/brighter.
Show a small tooltip with:
Block #1,287,421
Estimated: May 2033
Status: Available
Lock price: $1

The user can continue clicking different blocks without committing.

STEP 2 — USER SELECTS A BLOCK

When the user clicks a block, visually highlight it with a green ring.

Open/update the selected-block panel.

Show:

BLOCK #1,287,421

Estimated arrival
May 2033

50% likely range
March – July 2033

80% likely range
January – October 2033

Status
Available

Then show the CTA:

LOCK THIS BLOCK — $1

Below the button, add a small explanation:

Your prediction is permanent once locked.

Do NOT make the user feel like they have already claimed it.

They have only selected it.

STEP 3 — USER CAN CHANGE THEIR MIND

Before clicking the lock button, the user can continue exploring.

For example:

Block #1,287,421 → selected

Then they click:

Block #1,295,000

The selection changes to #1,295,000.

The previous block immediately returns to its normal available state.

The user can do this unlimited times before locking.

STEP 4 — USER CLICKS "LOCK THIS BLOCK"

When they click:

LOCK THIS BLOCK — $1

Open a confirmation modal.

Do NOT immediately charge them.

The modal should say:

LOCK YOUR PREDICTION?

Block #1,287,421

Estimated arrival:

May 2033

50% likely range:

March – July 2033

Then:

Once locked, this prediction cannot be changed or transferred.

Buttons:

← GO BACK

LOCK THIS BLOCK

Make the second button the obvious primary CTA.

STEP 5 — ACCOUNT CREATION

If the user isn't logged in, after confirming they want to lock the block, ask for their email.

Screen:

CREATE YOUR PREDICTION

Enter your email to save your prediction.

Email field:

you@example.com

Button:

CONTINUE

Use email verification or magic-link authentication.

Do not require a password if a magic-link flow is easy to implement.

Do not publicly display the user's email.

STEP 6 — FINAL PAYMENT / DEMO

For now, this is a DEMO PAYMENT FLOW.

Do NOT connect live Stripe.

Show:

LOCK BLOCK #1,287,421

$1.00

Your prediction:

#1,287,421

Estimated:

May 2033

Then:

LOCK MY BLOCK — $1

For the demo, clicking this should simulate a successful payment.

Clearly label the environment internally as TEST/DEMO.

STEP 7 — SUCCESS

After the demo payment succeeds, show a strong confirmation screen:

🔒 BLOCK LOCKED
#1,287,421

You picked the block.

Estimated arrival:

May 2033

Then show:

50% likely range
March – July 2033

80% likely range
January – October 2033

Add:

Your prediction is now permanently locked.

Then button:

VIEW MY BLOCK
STEP 8 — RETURN TO THE MAP

When the user clicks VIEW MY BLOCK:

Take them back to the map.

Their block should now be:

GREEN

with a strong green ring/glow.

Show:

YOUR BLOCK

when hovering/selecting it.

The selected-block panel should say:

YOUR PREDICTION

Block #1,287,421

🔒 LOCKED

Estimated arrival:

May 2033

STEP 9 — USER'S ACCOUNT

Add a simple MY PREDICTION link/account area.

When the user returns later, they should see:

YOUR PREDICTION

BLOCK #1,287,421

🔒 Locked

Estimated arrival:

May 2033

50% likely range:

80% likely range:

January – October 2033

And:

VIEW ON MAP

There should be NO "CHANGE PREDICTION" BUTTON.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT RULE

The user is allowed to browse and select unlimited blocks before locking.

But once they click the final confirmation and the demo payment succeeds:

THE BLOCK IS PERMANENTLY LOCKED.

They cannot:

Change it
Transfer it
Release it
Claim another block

The database must enforce this server-side.

VISUAL EXPERIENCE

The experience should feel like buying a seat on a highly desirable event:

Explore → Select → Review → Lock → Celebrate

Do not make it feel like a boring registration form.

The map should remain visible throughout as much as possible.

The user should always understand:

"This is the exact block I am about to claim."

And after completion:

"This is my block."

DO NOT BUILD YET

Do not implement:

Live Stripe
Dynamic pricing
Block trading
Prediction changes
Transfers
Auctions

Those will come later.

For now, make the selection → confirmation → account → demo payment → permanent lock → return to map experience work perfectly.
